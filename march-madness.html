NCAA Tournament Challenge

Fill out a bracket for the NCAA men's tournament. You score points for every team you correctly pick to advance, with bonus points for upsets. Most points wins.

Upsets

Upsets earn bonus points equal to the difference in seed. For example, if a #13 seed beats a #4 seed, you get 9 extra points (13 − 4 = 9) on top of the 2 points for the first-round win. Upset bonuses count in every round.

Tiebreaker: record first. If records are tied, total points in the championship game.
