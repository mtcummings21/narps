Hot Starts and False Starts - Week 1 Recap

Volume 9, Issue 1 · September 17, 2026

Football is back! There is nothing better than a crisp, fall afternoon and the smell of football in the air. From college campuses, to NFL stadiums, the 2026 football season is officially off the ground. The League of NARPS is excited to showcase the various teams in our league with the introduction of our official website. As you know, the website offers many insights into past seasons, franchise histories, all-time records, and many other aspects related to our fantasy football league. Now that our site is up and running, I plan on posting newsletters here rather than sending it via MailChimp. For the most part, these newsletters will keep the same format as before. You will find the current standings below and also a short recap of each team's Week 1 performance. I'm not sure how often I'll be able to post a newsletter, but my hope is to do so somewhat frequently. Anyways, enjoy this week's write-up and make sure to explore the other pages on the League of NARPS website. Best of luck to everyone as we continue to roll through the 2026 season! - The Commish

Team by Team

0-1

ACLiens — Luke Rapp

Caleb Williams erupted for 37.1 points, one of the best individual performances this week, and was the biggest boom by beating his projection by 20.7 points. However, the ACLiens still came away from Week 1 with a loss, an injury to star WR A.J. Brown, and a bagel from Colston Loveland.

1-0

Arabian Stallions — Walid Salameh

The Stallions are off to a strong start after a double digit loss season in 2025. D'Andre Swift made sure this team began their 2026 campaign with a win by scoring 31.9 points, beating his projection by 20.5 points.

0-1

Business as Usual — Travis West

Unfortunately, this team ran into the top scoring team in Week 1. However, even with the loss, Business as Usual currently sits in 6th place. This Eagles' fan could not resist the QB/TE stack of Jalen Hurts and Dallas Goedert. This team will hope to find their first win next week against the Lincoln Legends.

1-0

The Deplorables — Zach Sizemore

The reigning champ gets the season started off in the same fashion they finished 2025. Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Isiah Likely scored 80% of the team's points in Week 1, while Drake London, Davante Adams, Rico Dowdle, and Courtland Sutton combined for 13.8 points. Regardless, the Deplorables find ways to score points and win games.

1-0

The Fantasy Football Team — Aaron Burns

With a win in Week 1, the Fantasy Football Team now claims the longest active regular season winning streak (4). Jaxson Dart, Justin Jefferson, and DJ Moore propelled this team to victory in dominating fashion as they manhandled Prino's Powerhouse with a margin of victory by 54.3 points.

0-1

Free Agents — Z Hickman

Not necessarily a great performance nor a poor performance, the Free Agents were not able to secure a Week 1 win despite three rushing TDs and 144 rushing yards from King Henry. Certainly, this team will be considering a QB change as Drake Maye's lackluster performance against the Seahawks leaves much to be desired. Meanwhile, Patrick Mahomes is keeping the pine warm for the Free Agents.

1-0

George's Gangstas — Sonny Wright

Scoring 122.5 in Week 1, the Gangstas had their second best ever start to the season. Led by Kenneth Walker (32.6 pts) and Trevor Lawrence (26.1 pts), this team took care of business against the Free Agents, beating them for the tenth time out of sixteen head-to-head matchups. Can this team get back to its 2011 roots when they last won a title?

1-0

Kareem Pie — Seth Thacker

With newcomer Seth Thacker at the helm, the Piemakers cruised their way to victory in Week 1. Christian Watson popped off for 29.7 points while Bijan Robinson and David Montgomery each scored 27+ points, leading the way for the Piemakers to be the top scoring team of the week.

0-1

Lincoln Legends — Tyler Cummings

The Legends' 10-game win streak came to an end this week and they've already matched their loss total for 2025. With a projection of 16.4 points, Ja'Marr Chase was the biggest bust in Week 1 securing only two catches for twelve yards.

0-1

Money Badgers — Tyler Clay

Sitting at 100 career wins, the Badgers were unable to garner another win in Week 1. Ashton Jeanty (29.7 pts) and Trey McBride (20.0 pts) did not disappoint, but their fellow Badger teammates, Bo Nix and Marvin Harrison Jr., combined for only 9.2 points. This is only the fourth loss to the Ravers in sixteen matchups between the two teams.

0-1

Prino's Powerhouse — Joe Prino

In what was the third worst start of the season for this franchise, the Powerhouse will have a decision to make in Week 2 when it comes to the starting QB - Purdy or Herbert?. Not only does this team have a question mark at QB, the injuries to Brock Bowers and TreVeyon Henderson could derail the season before it leaves the station.

1-0

Route 2 Ravers — Joe Garton

Coming into this season, the Ravers were 4-10 all-time in Week 1. It was the first four picks of this team's 2026 draft (Jonathan Taylor, Javonte Williams, Breece Hall, and Bucky Irving) that led the Ravers to their fifth win on opening day. He should hit up Terry McLaurin to see if he plans on showing up in Week 2 after only scoring 2.4 points in Week 1.
