In Memory Of
Matt Geiger
League of NARPS · 2011–2025

Matt, each of us in this League were lucky to have you in our lives. Your love for your friends, sports, and life made you enjoyable to be around. You knew how to have fun with people while also understanding who they were inside.

In memory of you, I want to share a few of your team's fantasy history. Your deepest playoff run came in 2015, when you earned a first-round bye and finished 3rd. In your 15-year NARPS career you scored 18,539.7 points and made the playoffs 7 times. 2012 was your best regular season finish, going 9-3.

Unfortunately, your team was also on the wrong end of history. You are tied for the longest losing streak at 9. The biggest mark you made on the League is your draft selection of Andy Dalton in the 2nd round back in 2014. You were always a wild card, and this uniquely made you Geiger. We miss and love you.
