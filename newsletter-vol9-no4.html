Feast or Famine

Volume 9, Issue 4 · October 8, 2026

Week 4 of the NFL was a roller coaster of emotions for NARPS owners as we saw a large disparity between the higher and lower scoring teams. Notably, we had three teams post a score of 130+ points, led by the Powerhouse with a high score of 137.5 points. Conversely, we also saw three teams fail to reach the 70-point threshold, including a 69.2-68.5 barn burner won by the Money Badgers over the Lincoln Legends. The biggest story of Week 4 involved two referees who received suspensions for their in-game conduct. Fantasy fans can rest assured that the “stripes” were held accountable for their actions. As the NFL season starts to settle in, NARPS owners look to solidify their starting lineups in a season that has been riddled with injuries on all fronts.

Power Rankings

3-1

1. The Deplorables — Zach Sizemore

The Deplorables retain their top spot even after a slip-up against the Powerhouse. This team has scored the most points in the league and has remained consistent throughout the first four weeks of the season. The Deplorables look to keep the train rolling into Week 5 in a big matchup with another top squad, the Free Agents.

3-1

2. Free Agents — Z Hickman

The Free Agents are hot after posting an impressive win against the Fantasy Football Team in Week 4. With Tetairoa McMillan on bye, the Free Agents look to Zay Flowers and DK Metcalf to produce at WR against the top-ranked team in the league in Week 5.

3-1

3. Kareem Pie — Seth Thacker

This team has entered the league on a mission to prove it belongs, posting an impressive 3-1 record in the first quarter of the season. In one of the most surprising moves we’ve seen in league history, Kareem Pie managed to keep Jeremiyah Love on the roster after mistakenly dropping him before the Week 4 slate. With this rookie owner’s one mulligan behind him, does he have what it takes to make precision clicks when making roster moves as the season rolls on?

3-1

4. Prino’s Powerhouse — Joe Prino

The Powerhouse found their footing in Week 4 with a noteworthy win over the top-ranked team. With the injury bug behind them, the league staple will aim to keep the lights on for the rest of the season, starting in Week 5 against the Arabian Stallions.

2-2

5. Route 2 Ravers — Joe Garton

The Ravers are a scrappy bunch that can’t seem to stay down. After several disappointing seasons, it looks like their owner has the team trending upward. The Ravers will need big production from their workhorses, Jonathan Taylor and Javonte Williams, for their road matchup in Nebraska against the Legends.

1-3

6. Fantasy Football Team — Aaron Burns

Rounding out the top half of the Power Rankings is the Fantasy Football Team. Despite a positive All-Play record, this team finds itself at 1-3. Early injuries were not kind to this roster, highlighted by an early exit for their franchise QB, Jaxson Dart, in Week 2. Week 5 sets the table for a must-win matchup against Business as Usual, featuring two teams clinging to their last hopes of staying in the race for a top-5 finish.

2-2

7. George’s Gangstas — Sonny Wright

At 2-2, the Gangstas have dropped five spots in the Power Rankings, mostly due to untimely injuries to key players such as Saquon Barkley and DeVonta Smith. With both players out and Kenneth Walker III on bye, this team may have to resort to intimidation tactics to squeak out a Week 5 win against a solid Kareem Pie team.

1-3

8. Business as Usual — Travis West

Malik Nabers and Quinshon Judkins came alive for the first time this season in Week 4, paving the way for this team’s first win of the year. After their most explosive scoring week of the season, Business as Usual cannot let off the gas in a road matchup against the Fantasy Football Team. Both teams sit at 1-3, making this early-season matchup a must-win to stay in the playoff hunt.

2-2

9. Arabian Stallions — Walid Salameh

After a convincing win over the ACLiens in Week 4, the Stallions look to bolster their reputation in a Week 5 showing against a fully charged Powerhouse lineup. This matchup pits horsepower against modern electricity. The Stallions’ owner has a key decision to make at QB, with Dak Prescott currently riding the pine in favor of Matthew Stafford. Stafford did not look great last week, but maybe the Stallions’ owner knows something we don’t.

3-1

10. Money Badgers — Tyler Clay

It’s not often you see a 3-1 team sit this low in the Power Rankings, but here we are. The Money Badgers have continued to weasel their way to victory after victory this year, all while posting the third-lowest point total in the league. Defense seems to be this squad’s greatest strength, as they have held opponents to a league-best 339.2 points on the year. We’ll all be watching to see if the Money Badgers roll out the three-TE set against the ACLiens in Week 5.

1-3

11. Lincoln Legends — Tyler Cummings

The owner of the Lincoln Legends has to be scratching his head after a 1-3 start through the first four weeks of the season. Last year, this team put together one of the greatest regular seasons in NARPS history, only to fall short in the playoffs. This squad will attempt to prove the haters wrong in a pivotal Week 5 matchup against the Ravers. Will the result leave people wondering if last year was a fluke?

0-4

12. ACLiens — Luke Rapp

No surprise here. With a league-worst 0-4 record, the ACLiens have not been able to put it all together this season, despite a close matchup with the league’s top team in Week 1. The good news is that this team faces the Money Badgers in Week 5 and will rely on its combo of superstar WRs, JSN and Chris Olave, to get into the win column.

Record Watch

  • The 20,000 Club. Joe Prino became the first franchise in league history to reach 20,000 career points in Week 3, and Sonny Wright followed him in Week 4 (now at 20,006.6). It won’t be exclusive for long: Zach Sizemore needs just 25.4 points this week to join, and Tyler Cummings is 74.7 away.
  • Century marks. Joe Prino’s 137.5 in Week 4 was the 100th triple-digit game of his career, making him the fourth franchise to reach 100, and it was also his league-leading 27th weekly high score. Meanwhile, Zach Sizemore’s 101.9 pulled him into a tie with Tyler Clay for the most 100-point games ever (103). Sonny Wright sits at 99, one big week from joining the club.
  • Luke Rapp’s slide. Luke has lost eight straight dating back to Week 11 of last season. One more loss ties the league’s longest losing streak (9), shared by Matt Geiger (2014), Z Hickman (2018–19) and Aaron Burns (2022). Standing between Luke and history: Tyler Clay’s Money Badgers, coming off a 69.2-point week. Two years removed from a championship, Luke needs a win this week to stay out of the record books.

Site Updates

A few new features have been added to the website since the last issue:

  • The 2026 race charts are live. As promised, the 2026 Season page now has its Standings Race chart, which tracks where every team sits in the standings each week. It’s joined by a new Points Race chart that shows how far each team trails the league’s scoring leader week by week. Through Week 4, the Deplorables lead the league in points, with Free Agents 36.5 back and the bottom three teams more than 130 points behind.
  • Median and All-Play records on the All-Time Standings. Each franchise’s all-time median record and all-play record now sit next to its actual record, each with its own win %. The median record is your record if you played the league’s median score every week. The all-play record is your record if you played every team every week. Together they show who’s been lucky and who’s been robbed by the schedule over 16 seasons. (Walid and Aaron, I’m looking at you.)
  • Pick your columns. The All-Time Standings table has a new Columns menu in the top right. You can hide any stats you don’t care about, and the site will remember your choices the next time you visit.
  • Corrected playoff stats. The playoff byes and championship-game records on the All-Time Standings page are now calculated directly from each season’s playoff bracket. A few franchises had numbers that were off, and they’ve been fixed.
  • Easier to find the current season. The homepage now has a 2026 Season button front and center, and the 2026 standings box links straight to the full season page with scores, box scores and charts.
  • Survivor updates. Picks for Thursday night games are now revealed at kickoff on Thursday, while all other picks stay hidden until Sunday at 1 PM. Eliminated players now show an X for the remaining weeks.
