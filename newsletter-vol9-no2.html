A Generation Crippled — Week 2 Recap

Volume 9, Issue 2 · September 24, 2026

The historical scoring in Week 1 of the 2026 NFL season came to a halt in Week 2. In Week 1, there were 791 points scored across the NFL including 93 touchdowns (a NFL Week 1 record). That number fell to 647 points and 69 touchdowns in Week 2. Teams across the League of NARPS were not immune to this scoring dip, and the numbers back it up. NARPS' teams averaged 106.5 points in Week 1 and 91.1 points in Week 2.

Part of the scoring dry spell can be attributed to a generation that got crippled out on the playing field this past weekend. In the fourth quarter of the Vikings-Bears game, Caleb Williams went down in agony with a non-contact hamstring injury. Then, in the Sunday afternoon slate, Jayden Daniels' elbow tried to move in ways that are not natural for the human body. Finally, Monday Night Football gave us Jaxson Dart sustaining what looks like a serious knee injury. In one weekend, the 1st and 2nd overall picks of the 2024 NFL Draft and the 25th pick of the 2025 draft are all out for at least some period of time.

Week 2 LoN Recap

2-0

Route 2 Ravers — Joe Garton

The Ravers' QB, Jayden Daniels, went down with an injury that made even Tom Brady curse out loud. Despite losing him before halftime, the Ravers put in a team effort to take down the Gangstas in Week 2. Jonathan Taylor continued building on his record-setting 2025 season with a 27.2-point performance against the Chiefs. The Ravers will need Terry McLaurin and Matthew Golden to start producing soon if this team wants to make a playoff run. For now, the Ravers move to 2-0 and look to keep the momentum rolling into Week 3.

1-1

Prino's Powerhouse — Joe Prino

The Powerhouse benched Justin Herbert in favor of Brock Purdy, and the move paid off as Purdy scored 28.4 points while Herbert only managed 7.8. The Powerhouse was fortunate to draw the Piemakers this week, having failed to crack 80 points for the second straight week. Brock Bowers is hopeful to play in Week 3, and TreVeyon Henderson returned in Week 2 to put up 13.6 points, but the injury bug continues to plague this team as Puka Nacua missed this past week and may miss the upcoming one too. The Powerhouse only needs 64.7 points to become the first franchise to reach 20,000 points scored in the regular season.

1-1

Money Badgers — Tyler Clay

In Week 2, fresh off the waiver wire, Tyler Shough got the start for the Money Badgers and put up a solid 22.3 points. CMC did not disappoint, putting up a 20-burger. The two TEs seem to be working out thus far as both Tyler Warren and Trey McBride found the end zone this past week. Speaking of the Cardinals, did anyone see Marvin Harrison Jr.? This guy needs a complete career reset. His 0 points in Week 2 now mark his fourth straight regular-season game without recording multiple receptions. Meanwhile, Jeanty was a little hobbled against the Chargers and Alec Pierce left the game against the Chiefs. This team seems to be stumbling and was fortunate its opponent's QB left the game injured, securing a much-needed win for the Badgers.

1-1

Lincoln Legends — Tyler Cummings

After the Legends' 10-game win streak came to an end last week, they were able to secure their 100th career win in Week 2. Ja'Marr Chase used his miraculous footwork to score his first TD of the season and added his second TD later in the game. Dalton Kincaid is currently TE1 in fantasy and scored 19.0 points this past Thursday night. Chuba Hubbard seems to be finding his way as the lead back for the Panthers and Legends, having now found the end zone three times this season. If this team wants to continue to win, they'll need more consistency from Rashee Rice and George Pickens along with better outputs from Lamar Jackson.

1-1

Kareem Pie — Seth Thacker

After scoring the most points in Week 1, this team was the lowest scorer in Week 2. I think Week 2 is more of a fluke for this team, as Bijan Robinson only scored 9.6 and David Montgomery was limited to 3.4. Jeremiyah Love will look to get going in Week 3 after a disappointing Week 2. The Piemakers benched Jaylen Waddle after a piss poor performance in the season opener, but that move backfired as Waddle ended up with 17.8 points. I'm sure the starting lineup will continue to be refined as we progress through this season, and I expect this team to be more competitive than what they showed this past week.

1-1

George's Gangstas — Sonny Wright

Last week, the Gangstas were bolstered by Kenneth Walker's monster Week 1 performance. In Week 2, this team came back down to earth. To make matters worse, Saquon Barkley left the game injured and Trevor Lawrence put up a stinker in Denver. DeVonta Smith showcased his new role as the number-one receiver for the Eagles by hauling in 10 catches for 117 yards and 1 TD. With the injury to Caleb Williams, Gangstas WR Luther Burden will probably take a hit in his production. I don't want to say this team is in freefall, but I want to see some results over the next few weeks to buy into what they're selling.

1-1

Free Agents — Z Hickman

The Free Agents secured their first victory of 2026. No player dominated in Week 2, but the consistency across the board allowed this team to cruise to a win. Mahomes connected with Kelce for a TD, and Derrick Henry continued his strong start this year with another 16.2 points. Though the Free Agents used a 6th round pick on Drake Maye, they already benched last year's MVP runner-up for Patrick Mahomes. So far the move has paid off. This team could have bolstered their roster in the 6th round by taking Jaylen Warren, Mike Evans, or Christian Watson, but this team is finding its groove as we head into Week 3.

1-1

The Fantasy Football Team — Aaron Burns

The Football Team was on its way to a 2-0 start until Jaxson Dart was hurt on the Giants' first drive on Monday Night Football. With Dart leaving the game, the Football Team lost by 3.5 points. James Cook showcased his talents in the Thursday night game against the Lions by rushing for 135 yards and finding the end zone. Meanwhile, Cook's teammate, DJ Moore, left the game injured with a -0.1 fantasy score. Garrett Wilson, George Kittle, and Stefon Diggs all scored 14+ points in the Football Team's Week 2 loss and will need to help carry this team as they sort through some injuries.

2-0

The Deplorables — Zach Sizemore

The 2025 champion is off to the best start so far in 2026. Just like last week, Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs powered this team to victory with both scoring 40.7 and 20.3, respectively. In Week 1, it was Isaiah Likely who popped off; however, in Week 2 it was Davante Adams that came through by scoring 35.5 points on Monday night. The Deplorables secured their 21st career weekly scoring title, which now puts them in a tie for third place. I do not see this team slowing down, and now that Penix is coming back, Drake London should see a spike in production. Watch out NARPS, the Deplorables are coming in to wreck the league again in 2026.

0-2

Business as Usual — Travis West

Nothing worse than an 0-2 start to the fantasy season. Malik Nabers played through an injury on Monday night and had to work with backup Jameis Winston, ending up scoring only 0.6 points. Dallas Goedert left the game in the 2nd quarter and has been diagnosed with a sprained MCL. Rhamondre Stevenson only scored 3.1 points for Business as Usual as TreVeyon Henderson returned to action this past week. The spotlight belongs to CeeDee Lamb, who had a monstrous game and scored 31.3 points. I do think Quinshon Judkins will see better days ahead once the Browns QB situation is settled (though their QB situation has been a problem for decades). This team will be desperate for a win in Week 3.

1-1

Arabian Stallions — Walid Salameh

The Stallions were the unfortunate team that had to face the Deplorables in Week 2 and took the L even though they scored the second most points. Dak Prescott and Amon-Ra St. Brown combined for over 60 points. Meanwhile, Omarion Hampton and D'Andre Swift each crossed double-digits and will help anchor this team going forward. The core of this team is one of the better ones in the League of NARPS, and I expect the Stallions to make a strong playoff push.

0-2

ACLiens — Luke Rapp

Well, this team is living up to its injury prone name. AJ Brown placed on IR, Caleb Williams and JK Dobbins exit their games with hamstring injuries, and Caleb Douglas left the game with an ankle injury. Six of eight players scored less than 8 points, and five of those players scored less than 5. There are currently only two bright spots on this team, JSN and Chris Olave. These two are a deadly fantasy combo and will need to carry the rest of the team on their backs. Somehow, this team will try to find a better supporting cast moving into Week 3 as they seek to end their 6-game losing streak.
