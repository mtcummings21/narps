Super Bowl Squares

Every square purchased is entered into a random draw to set its place on the 10 × 10 grid. The two Super Bowl teams are then assigned to the X and Y axes, and the numbers 0 through 9 are drawn at random for each team's ten slots.

How to Win

At the end of every quarter, take the last digit of each team's score. The square where those two numbers meet on the grid wins that quarter.

Overtime: If the game is tied at the end of the 4th quarter, the final square is decided by the final score after overtime (rule implemented after SB LVIII).
