Happy Thanksgiving

Volume 9, Issue 9 · November 26, 2026

Happy Thanksgiving, my fellow NARPS! I hope everyone has a wonderful day with family, friends, food, and football.

We have a tight race for the two bye spots, and the 6th place spot remains competitive. This week I have some quick hitters for each team, and I hope to have a more in-depth newsletter next week. Only three weeks remain, which is enough time for some teams to move up or down in the standings as we race toward the end of the regular season.

Best of luck to everyone, and I hope you enjoy this week’s newsletter!

Quick Hitters

1st · 8-3

George’s Gangstas

The Gangstas are on the rise, climbing from 5th place in Week 8 to 1st place after last week. This team is 3rd in total points scored for the season, but they have yet to be the top-scoring team in any week. Regardless, the Gangstas are in pole position to secure a first-round bye.

2nd · 7-4

The Deplorables

After scoring 151.3 in Week 10, the Deplorables turned in their worst performance of the season in Week 11. They drop to 2nd place but have been in 1st or 2nd since Week 2. Is this team coming back down to earth, or will they return to their high-scoring ways and lock up a first-round bye?

3rd · 7-4

Prino’s Powerhouse

The Powerhouse has slipped a spot in the standings in each of the past two weeks, and the outlook continues to dim. Puka Nacua is out for the season, TreVeyon Henderson is out with health issues, and Kyren Williams and Jaylen Warren have cooled off in recent weeks. This team still has enough depth to stay competitive, but the firepower has certainly been dampened.

4th · 7-4

Kareem Pie

The Piemakers’ three-game win streak ended last week when they failed to reach 80 points. Though they sit 4th in the standings, they are 8th in points scored and 6th in All-Play record. With their byepocalypse over, Kareem Pie will hope to get back on track in Week 12.

5th · 6-5

Free Agents

The Free Agents have held the 5th place spot for three straight weeks. They finally broke triple digits in Week 11 after three consecutive weeks under 100. Will this team finally bench DK Metcalf after a pathetic 2026 campaign?

6th · 6-5

Fantasy Football Team

The Fantasy Football Team is just one game above .500, yet much to my surprise, they boast the top All-Play record in the league. FFT has yet to dip below 7th place in the standings and will hope to stay in the top 6 to secure a postseason bid.

7th · 4-7

Route 2 Ravers

The Ravers have won two of their last three matchups and sit just 8.1 points behind 6th place. Breece Hall’s return from injury has given the Ravers a much-needed boost, especially with Jonathan Taylor scoring double digits only once in the past four weeks. The Ravers will fight to get back to 6th place, where they’ve spent most of this season.

8th · 4-7

Lincoln Legends

The Legends were at the bottom of the standings in Week 9, but two strong weeks have lifted them to 8th place. Just two weeks ago they were 93 points behind 6th place; now they’re only 29 points back. A recent trade with the Arabian Stallions may be just the juice this team needed to make one last push for the playoffs.

9th · 5-6

Business as Usual

Business as Usual’s four-game win streak came to an end in Week 11. At 5-6, this team can still make a run at a playoff spot, but their 4-7 median record suggests they’ve benefited from their schedule. Business as Usual hasn’t cracked the top seven in the standings since Week 1, and there doesn’t seem to be much hope they’ll do so again by season’s end.

10th · 6-5

Money Badgers

At 6-5, the Badgers keep hanging around as they chase a postseason appearance, despite ranking 10th in scoring and 9th in All-Play record. Badgers opponents haven’t reached 100 points in the past four weeks, a streak the Ravers will hope to snap in Week 12. This team will need to win and hope the other 6-5 teams lose to make the playoffs.

11th · 3-8

Arabian Stallions

It’s been a rough 2026 for the Stallions. This team hasn’t scored 100+ points since Week 2, and last week’s score would have gotten a boost if newly acquired WR Mike Evans had been in the starting lineup. At this point, the Stallions are playing to pad their all-time records. There will be no hardware in 2026.

12th · 3-8

ACLiens

This legendary franchise will be scratching their heads when they look back on 2026. Unfortunate injuries and draft picks have been this team’s undoing. The ACLiens are on a four-game losing streak and will hope to end it against the Deplorables.
