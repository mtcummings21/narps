The First 2026 Power Rankings

Volume 9, Issue 3 · October 1, 2026

It’s time for the first 2026 Power Rankings! Teams are starting to take shape and it’s time to analyze where each team stands. My plan is to do a Power Rankings at the conclusion of each month. So now that September is behind, it’s time to see where your team stands after the first calendar month of the season has concluded. I will be taking a break from posting a newsletter next week, but the website will continue to be updated.

Power Rankings

Tier 1

3-0

1. The Deplorables — Zach Sizemore

This team has come out the gates and taken a noticeable lead in the race. The Deplorables are the last remaining undefeated team and are on a record setting scoring pace through three games. Their all-play record is 29-4 which is eight more wins than the next highest team. Led by Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs it’s going to be hard to catch this team. Especially when Davante Adams and Drake London are putting out great statlines. Oh, and now Sizemore’s RB, Bhayshul Tuten, is being gifted TDs by Trevor Lawrence. The Deplorables are the clear favorite to win the championship this year.

Tier 2

2-1

2. George’s Gangstas — Sonny Wright

After being dismissed by the press last week, the Gangstas have made their way to the 2nd spot in the Power Rankings. This team is anchored by Kenneth Walker and is complemented by Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith, Luther Burden, Ladd McConkey, and Parker Washington. And if Trevor Lawrence decides to run in the endzone himself rather than taking a knee at the one yard line, then the Gangstas could make a run for the top spot. I think this team has a solid floor to work with each week and certainly has upside from nearly every starter in the lineup. The bench depth is solid and will help should this team incur injuries in the future.

2-1

3. Kareem Pie — Seth Thacker

The Piemakers have had a solid start to the 2026 campaign and last week Bijan Robinson put the league on alert by scoring 34.3 points. I’m not sure I can get behind Bryce Young but he is currently QB4 for the year and this team can always pivot back to Joe Burrow if needed. I do expect to see some inconsistencies from this team as David Montgomery and Jaylen Waddle continue to settle into their new teams. Jeremiyah Love had his best week last week as the rookie continues to adjust to the NFL. Kyle Pitts continues to baffle fantasy owners after having only one catch for five yards in Week 3. If Nico Collins can get healthy and if the Texans can actually produce points, then the Piemakers will hope to find more production in their lineup.

1-2

4. Fantasy Football Team — Aaron Burns

Though this team boasts the 2nd best all-play record (just barely), they are 1-2 on the season. This team has been dealing with a host of injuries including DJ Moore, Justin Jefferson, Travis Etienne, and Jonathon Brooks. This team currently rosters three TEs (along with the Gangsta’s) and two of them fall outside the top 20. It’s no wonder this League does not want to get rid of the TE position. Anyways, the one glaring weakness of this team for me is the QB position. I’m not sold on Jordan Love and the Packers (sorry Joey G). Love is currently QB15 on the year. If the Fantasy Football Team wants to get back to the championship match this year, bolstering their QB could go a long way.

2-1

5. Prino’s Powerhouse — Joe Prino

First, I want to congratulate the Powerhouse on becoming the first NARPS’ franchise to reach 20,000 regular season career points. For 2026, the Powerhouse got off to a slow start after dealing with injuries to Brock Bowers and Puka Nacua. With Bowers returning in Week 3, this team turned things around and scored 127.4 points. When healthy, I don’t see any weaknesses on this team. Brock Purdy is having himself an MVP season through the first three games. Puka is returning and his Powerhouse and real life teammate, Kyren Williams, has provided solid scoring so far. Jaylen Warren continues to be a wrecking ball and Tee Higgins once again reminds us he is one of the best #2 receivers in the NFL. Now, the Powerhouse will hope TreVeyon Henderson can get rolling again and if so, I expect this team to rise many spots in the next Power Rankings.

2-1

6. Free Agents — Z Hickman

The Free Agents finish the list for tier 2 of the Power Rankings. This team is 2nd in points scored (by 0.7 points) and has an 18-15 all-play record. De’Von Achane has been sent to the free agents by the Free Agents after his season ending injury. This week, the Free Agents acquired former free agent, Ollie Gordon, to take his place. The current starting lineup consists of four AFC North players, Derrick Henry, DK Metcalf, Zay Flowers, and Harold Fannin. Out of those four, it's the Steelers player that will cause heartache for this team. Luckily, the Free Agents drafted Patrick Mahomes who has salvaged this season after an atrocious start by Drake Maye. And with Travis Kelce in his husband era, the TE2 on the season has found his groove once again.

Tier 3

1-2

7. Arabian Stallions — Walid Salameh

The Stallions are one of four teams to have scored 300 points so far, yet this team has a 16-17 all-play record. Amon-Ra St. Brown and D’Andre Swift are two studs this team will continue to rely on for points. Meanwhile, the youngsters crew of Omarion Hampton, Rome Odunze, and Cam Skattebo should help lift this team off the ground. The biggest question mark for the Stallions is Brian Thomas Jr. who has yet to break six points in a game this season. Otherwise, this team is solidly in the middle of the pack and will hope to break into the tier above.

2-1

8. Route 2 Ravers — Joe Garton

The Route 2 Ravers have won two of the first three matchups in 2026 and find themselves right in the mix. This team’s strength is in its RBs consisting of Jonathan Taylor, Javonte Williams, Bucky Irving, and Breece Hall. All four of these RBs are in the top 20 for fantasy. Matthew Golden is finally providing quality production which most Packers fans were hoping to see last year. I’m not necessarily a big believer in Terry McLaurin, but he is coming off a 16.7 point performance in Week 3. If McLaurin can provide consistency, then the Ravers will be a tough team to contend with as we progress deeper into the season.

2-1

9. Money Badgers — Tyler Clay

On paper, this team looks solid rostering five players that are in the top ten in scoring for their position. However, the Badgers have yet to cross the century mark in a matchup this season. I thought long and hard about if this team should be in the bottom tier especially when you considered their starting WRs, Emeka Egbuka and Keenan Allen. But taking a look at the whole roster, I’m convinced this team will start to turn it around here soon (hopefully after Week 4 since I’m playing him). CMC and Ashton Jeanty are two workhorses that are RB7 and RB8, respectively. Tyler Shough is QB3, just as we all predicted, and Trey McBride is TE1 which comes as no surprise. I would not be surprised to see this team move up in the next Power Rankings, but a lot will depend on how their starting WRs fare.

Tier 4

1-2

10. Lincoln Legends — Tyler Cummings

Ahhhh, the Legends. By far my favorite NARPS team to root for. So far, Ja’Marr Chase and Dalton Kincaid have been the only highlights to cheer for this season. Chase is coming off back to back 20 point weeks and seems to be finding his groove. Meanwhile, Jadarian Price continues to falter and is losing trust with the Legends front office by the day. Chuba Hubbard is currently RB9 and has been the only RB to do anything on this team. This team has potential with the likes of Lamar Jackson, Rashee Rice, and George Pickens, but they will have to produce soon if this team wants to be taken seriously. For now, the Legends are thankful there are two worst teams.

0-3

11. ACLiens — Luke Rapp

We are finally getting to the bottom of the barrel where desperation is high and hope is starting to dwindle. Many thought the ACLiens were back after their championship run in 2024, but that seems to be misguided optimism. The season really could not have gotten off to a worse start. AJ Brown is hurt in Week 1. Caleb Williams goes down in Week 2 and Colston Loveland finally scored more than 1 point in a game. I do think the ACLiens have the best 1-2 combo at WR with Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Chris Olave, but they are not enough to overcome the deficiencies for this team. JK Dobbins and Braelon Allen are the starting RBs and Josh Downs and Jameson Williams are the starting FLEX players. This does not bode too much confidence. Maybe this team can make a run once AJ Brown and Caleb Williams are fully healthy.

0-3

12. Business as Usual — Travis West

The only usual thing about this team is their consistency at being near the bottom. Business as Usual has the least amount of points scored and has the worst all-play record of 7-26. When I look at this roster, it does not seem they should be here at the bottom. Chase Brown and Quinshon Judkins have been disappointing so far. Malik Nabers has been working through a minor injury and Rhamondre Stevenson and Patriots have yet to get going. CeeDee Lamb is averaging 20 points per game and will hope to spark his Business as Usual teammates to do the same. Not all hope is lost as Kalif Raymond and Denzel Boston help solidify the depth of this roster. This team will hope to turn things around in Week 4 against the Gangstas.
