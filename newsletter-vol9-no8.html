The Great Escape

Volume 9, Issue 8 · November 12, 2026

Alright folks, we have another issue of the Couch Chronicles for your enjoyment. I’ve written some quick hitters for each team. Since I’m in charge, I decided to list the teams in reverse order of the standings. Five more weeks to go!

In This Issue

  1. Quick Hitters
  2. Week 9 Standings
  3. Points Race

Quick Hitters

2-7

Lincoln Legends

This year is proof that things do balance out over time (usually). The Legends went 13-1 last year and now we sit at 2-7 and tied for last place. A brief name change to Mario Brothers only made things worse a couple weeks ago when Lamar left the game on the first drive. The best thing about last place is that there is no place to go but up. This team now holds the longest current losing streak with 4 straight losses.

3-6

ACLiens

Whatever hope existed two weeks ago as this team seemed to be nearing full health and ready to make a push has now evaporated just like Republicans in Congress after the midterms. Tied for last with his former Moses Little League teammate, the ACLiens are in a fight to avoid the dreaded 12th place spot.

4-5

Money Badgers

This team now has three straight losses and any hope of making a postseason run is fading as quickly as the sun these days with the switch to Standard time. This roster makes you raise an eyebrow and the stats back it up. Last week, only two starters surpassed 10 points. Yikes. Can this team turn things around in Week 10 as they face their former Cabell Midland classmate?

3-6

Arabian Stallions

The Stallions have a 3.2 point advantage over the Legends and ACLiens and a 2.4 point lead over the Badgers, which slots them in 9th place. The Stallions left the gate quickly at the start of the season, but the lack of water in the Arabian desert has left this team high and dry. The Stallions will need to find a way to fend off the bottom three dwellers in order to avoid their third last place finish.

4-5

Business as Usual

Who was it that wrote just two weeks ago, “I expect this team to dig out of the hole it created and to make a serious push for the 6th and final playoff spot.” Oh wait. That was me. Ok, enough tooting my own horn, but this team has now won three in a row. Business as Usual now sits one game back from 5th place and has closed the gap from 6th place by nearly 10 pts. That being said, Josh Jacobs and Rhamondre Stevenson are hurt and Chase Brown still needs to get going if this team wants to continue riding its recent wave of success.

5-4

Fantasy Football Team

Someone once wrote the following about this team, “By the time we get to the end of this season, I would bet there’s a better chance last year’s runner-up won’t be in the playoffs.” It seems whoever wrote this may have been on to something as the Fantasy Football Team went from 4th to 7th place last week. But, future weeks could go better for the FFT. Justin Jefferson is getting his old QB back, and Garrett Wilson should return from injury. We still do not know when/if Travis Etienne will return from IR and this team desperately needs RB depth (who doesn’t?). Week 10 provides a chance for the FFT to silence their biggest critic as they face the 2-7 Lincoln Legends.

3-6

Route 2 Ravers

It’s been six straight weeks that this team has been holding onto 6th place. Oh, and during most of that span, the Ravers were on a six-game losing streak. Regardless, this team does not quit and is currently 4th in points scored on the season. The schedule this year has been brutal and unfortunate, but I still hold my conviction that this team will qualify for the 6th place spot.

5-4

Free Agents

The Free Agents got stomped in Week 9 losing by 60.1, but they still find themselves in a playoff spot. Since Week 2, this team has not dipped below 7th place. Now that Jacoby Brissett is a Raven, this could be a major boost for Zay Flowers. The next five weeks will be a grind for the Agents.

6-3

George’s Gangstas

The Gangstas do not panic even when their season could have gone a different way. Instead, they have gone from 8th place in Week 6 to 4th place in Week 9 and they sit only one game back from first. KWIII still continues to lift this team, but they have enough playmakers to take on almost any opponent. Teams should be on alert anytime the Gangstas come rollin’ through their hood.

6-3

Kareem Pie

The Piemakers have never delivered so many tasty pies in their history, and it could not come at a better time with Thanksgiving around the corner. Oh, wrong kind of pie. Anyways, this team could be one of the top dawgs by the end of the season now that Burrow is ballin’. Bijan Robinson and Christian Watson are ready to take on any NARPS that comes their way. It may be this owner’s first year with the NARPS, but it’s clear they bring a strong pedigree to the League.

6-3

The Deplorables

Suddenly, the Deplorables are no longer the dominant team which I was not expecting just a few weeks ago. Regardless, they enter Week 10 with the second most points and a 6-3 record. They took down their cousin last week in what was a lackluster matchup. Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs underperformed, and the Deplorables are ready to crush their remaining opponents.

7-2

Prino’s Powerhouse

Bry asked me this week why Prino’s team is always so good and I hate that’s the question she asked me about our League. I’d like to think the answer is luck (that’s what I told her), but being dominant for 16 years requires more than luck. Unfortunately for this team, they will be without Puka Nacua for a few weeks as he heals from an injury. But the firepower on this team will allow the Powerhouse to compensate until they get their first-rounder back to action. It may be 2026 and our 16th year of the League, but with all that time some things have not changed. Will the Powerhouse win their 5th championship this year? I don’t even want to think about having to write that newsletter.
